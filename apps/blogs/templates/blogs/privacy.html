Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 • INKSPACE Blogging Platform

At INKSPACE, we take privacy, transparency, and data integrity seriously. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our Django MVT blogging platform, how that information is stored, how our AI Assistant processes requests, and how we protect your content.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect the information necessary to provide you with a clean, functional, and secure blogging experience:

  • Account Information: When you register on INKSPACE, we collect your full name, email address, chosen username, and password.
  • Profile Information: You may optionally provide a personal biography, professional designation, and an avatar photo to display on your author card.
  • Published and Draft Blog Content: When you write on INKSPACE, we store your article titles, descriptions, categories, full body content, and uploaded cover image files.
  • Follow Relationships: We record which authors you choose to follow in order to manage your relationship feeds.

2. How Your Information is Used

We use the data collected strictly for the following platform purposes:

  • To authenticate and manage your author sessions securely.
  • To display your published stories, author attribution, and reading time estimates publicly.
  • To save private drafts that only you can view, edit, or delete.
  • To prevent spam, abuse, and automated attacks via Django's security middleware.

3. Data Storage & Security Practices

INKSPACE adheres to standard Django security best practices:

  • Password Security: We never store plaintext passwords. All user passwords are encrypted using strong cryptographic hashing algorithms (PBKDF2 with SHA-256).
  • CSRF Protection: All form submissions and state-changing actions (such as blog creation, edits, deletion, and following) require cryptographically signed Django CSRF tokens.
  • Owner-Only Authorization: We enforce strict backend authorization rules ensuring only the verified author of a blog post can edit, update, or delete their stories.
  • Database Storage: Application data is persisted in a relational SQLite database (db.sqlite3) on the server.

4. AI Assistant & External API Processing

INKSPACE integrates an **AI Assistant** to help authors generate blog ideas, catchy titles, short summaries, and structured article outlines.

  • External Processing: When you submit a topic or prompt to the AI Assistant, that text is sent securely to the official Google Gemini API via server-side Django views.
  • Credential Safety: Your account passwords, private profile data, or unrelated articles are never sent to external AI services. Only the topic prompt you enter is processed.
  • Human Review Required: AI-generated content is returned solely to your editor or chatbot widget as a suggestion. It is never automatically published without your explicit review and confirmation.

5. User Responsibilities

As an author on INKSPACE, you are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials. You agree not to upload content that violates copyright laws, contains malicious software, or infringes upon the intellectual property rights of others.

6. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically as new platform features are introduced. Any changes will be reflected directly on this page with an updated timestamp.

Questions Regarding Privacy?

If you have any questions or concerns regarding our privacy practices or data handling, please reach out to our team.
